# 00 Daily Scratchpad

Your post-it. One note a day (`YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md`), blank, written by you, deliberately messy. Quick captures land here too. Nothing is filed by writing it here, and nothing has to be: carry a line on when it matters (Objects turns a line into a project, a person or a note; your AI can help), or leave it as it is. It stays forever, unmarked, and no AI ever edits it.
