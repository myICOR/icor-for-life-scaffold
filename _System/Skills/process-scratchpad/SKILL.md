# Process the Daily Notes

The scratchpad is `00 Daily Notes/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (the day's note) and the quick scratch notes `YYYYMMDDHHmm.md` in the same month folder. It is the person's raw record: **you never edit it**, you never mark it, and nothing in it has to go anywhere. Processing is optional: do it when they ask.

1. **Read the day** they name (today when they name none). Ask before you look further back.
2. **Suggest, piece by piece**, what is worth carrying (skill **capture** decides where):
   - a thing that happened or a thought, with that day as its identity → a journal entry (skill **write-journal-entry**; their words verbatim under `## Original Text`)
   - knowledge about a subject → a note in `02 Inner World/Notes/`
   - a fact about a person → a property on their contact note
   - a new project, goal, topic, habit → skill **new-object** (ask first; these are commitments)
   - a task → tell them; it belongs in their task app (the Planner plugin syncs it into `06 Planner/`)
   - anything else stays where it is, and that is fine In Obsidian they can do the same themselves: Objects turns a line into an object ("Make this line an object").
3. **Link everything** you create to its project, Key Element, topic or person, and to the day.
4. **Report** in a short list: what you created, what you updated, and any question last.
