# ICOR for Life

Your life and your work in one folder of plain markdown files, organised by ICOR (Input, Control, Output, Refine).
Everything is ready in Obsidian, and any AI you point at the folder knows how to work with you here.

## Start here

1. **Turn on the plugins.** Obsidian asks once whether you trust this folder's plugins: choose to trust them. They
   are the ICOR for Life plugins, made by myICOR.
2. **Write today.** Click the calendar icon (Open today's daily note) and write whatever is on your mind. Type `@` to
   link a person, project, topic or book as you write, or create it on the spot: `@person/Paco Cantero`,
   `@project/Learn Spanish`, `@book/Deep Work`. The Connections panel on the right shows the day line by line, each
   line ready to become a journal entry, a project or a note.
3. **Open your Journal** with the book icon: your entries as cards, your streak, and a question for today.
4. **Create something** with the shapes icon (New object): a goal, a project, a person, a book you are reading. You
   fill in only what it needs, and it lands in the right place. Missing a kind? `@tool` (or any word) adds it as a
   type of your own.
6. **Find your objects** in `05 Databases/Objects.base`: all of them in one table, and a view per type (Projects, People, your
   Tools…). Each type's folder has its own table with all its fields. Delete one with the command Delete this object.
5. **Bring in your AI.** Open this folder in Claude, Codex, Cursor, Gemini or another AI that works with files. It
   reads `AGENTS.md` and knows the folders, the rules and the skills.

## The folders, by ICOR stage

| Stage | Folder | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input | `00 Daily Notes` | your post-it: one note a day, deliberately messy, and quick scratch notes |
| Input | `01 Inbox` | scans and anything not yet sorted |
| Control | `02 Inner World` | what went through you: Journal, Notes, Contacts, My Life |
| Control | `03 Outer World` | what others made: books, articles, videos, podcasts, with your highlights |
| Control | `04 Assets` | images, audio and documents |
| Control | `05 Databases` | the tables: `Objects.base` (every object), and data your notes cannot hold |
| Output | `06 Planner` | your tasks, synced by the Planner plugin |

Refine has no folder: you refine with the plugins (AI Chat, Connect, Focus) and in your weekly review.

**My Life** (in `02 Inner World`) is the map of your life: Key Elements, Goals, Projects, Topics and Habits.
Everything you write connects to something on it, so you can find it again.

**Only what lasts goes in.** Work in progress stays in the tools where you do it; the result comes in when it is done.

The notes tagged `example` show how each kind of note looks. Keep them as long as they help, then delete them.

## Good to know

- **Your files stay yours.** Plain text in a folder on your computer: no account, no database, no lock-in. Back it up
  like any folder; Obsidian Sync, iCloud and Dropbox all carry it.
- **The look:** Settings, ICOR for Life - Interface: Gradient or Flat, and where a note's properties show (on top,
  folded, under the note, or only in the right sidebar).
- **`_System`** holds the structure, templates, skills and tools your AI and the plugins use. It is hidden in the file
  tree and kept out of search and the quick switcher; ICOR for Life - Interface shows it.
