# 03 Outer World

What others made: one note per book, article, video, podcast, post or paper (`type: source`), with your highlights. The Inner World is what went through you; this is what came from outside.

- **Clip from the browser.** Install the Obsidian Web Clipper, then in its settings Import template and pick `Web Clipper template.json` from this folder. Every clip lands here as a source. Write one line of why you kept it (`my_thought`).
- **Sync your highlights from Readwise.** See `Readwise/README.md`.
- **Connect it.** A source earns its place by linking to what it serves: a project, a Key Element, a topic. Your own thinking about it is a journal entry or a note in `02 Inner World` that links back here.
- **Your reading queue** is every source with `consumed: false`. Open `Sources.base`.
