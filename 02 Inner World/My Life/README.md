# My Life

The map of your life, in five parts:

- **Key Elements**: the big areas of your life (Health, Family, Work, Finances)
- **Goals**: outcomes you want, in a Key Element
- **Projects**: work with an end; every project serves a goal
- **Topics**: subjects you follow over time
- **Habits**: what you keep doing

Keep these notes short: what it is, why it matters, where it stands. Everything about it links here.
