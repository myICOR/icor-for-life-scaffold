# 08 Workbench

Where you work. One folder per piece of work, dated: `2026-11-28 Webinar outline/`. Anything goes inside: drafts, canvases, sketches, research, files your AI makes. It is the one place where messy is the point, so the rest of the folder can stay clean.

Every work folder starts with `brief.md`:

- **What** this is, in a line or two.
- **For** the My Life item it serves, as a link: `[[Learn Spanish]]`.
- **Next** the next step, for you or for your AI. Write it before you stop, and your AI can carry on while you are away.

When the work is done, what lasts leaves as objects (a note, a journal entry, a project update, a source) and the folder moves to `Done/`. Nothing is deleted. Tasks stay in `07 Planner`.
