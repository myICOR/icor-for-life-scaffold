# Journal

Your entries, in `YYYY/MM/`, named `YYYY-MM-DD_<slug>.md`. Each is one of four types: **interaction** (with another person), **note** (something noticed), **thought** (real thinking) or **milestone** (a threshold crossed). Your words go under `## Original Text` and are never changed; `## AI Expansion` is for what your AI adds. Open the Journal from the book icon, or write one with its + button.
