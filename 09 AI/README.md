# 09 AI

Your AI's own record. Whichever AI you point at this folder (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini or the next one) works inside it and keeps its notes here, so the record stays with you when you change tools.

| Here | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `Memory.md` | what your AI has learned about how you work: preferences, decisions, corrections. It reads this first and adds to it when you correct it. Edit it any time. |
| `Log/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` | what was done in this folder, one file a day: when, which AI, where, what changed. Earlier entries are never edited. |
| `Reviews/YYYY/YYYY-Www.md` | the weekly reviews: what happened, what worked, what to refine, the next week's priorities. |

Your Daily Notes are what you wrote. The Log is what your AI did. Read both in the weekly review: that is the Refine in ICOR.

`AGENTS.md`, in the top folder, is where every AI starts reading. It sends them here.
